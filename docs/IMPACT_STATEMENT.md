# Draft 2030 Impact Statement — Academic Readiness Coach

**Ivy Tech Community College — Academic & Instructional Quality**
**Statewide Strategy: Optimize Student Placement & Academic Readiness**

---

## Overview

This document describes the anticipated 2030 impact of a scaled deployment of the Academic Readiness Coach system at Ivy Tech Community College. All projections are **aspirational targets** based on research literature and institutional reasoning. They are **not measured outcomes** from this prototype, which was developed during the 2026 IT Challenge using fictional sample data.

---

## The Gap We Are Addressing

Co-requisite math enrollment places underprepared students directly into gateway college-level courses alongside embedded remediation. Research consistently shows that early intervention — in the first two to three weeks of a semester — has the greatest positive effect on gateway course success rates (Complete College America, 2016; Bailey et al., 2015).

The gap is **information asymmetry**: students lack timely, actionable knowledge about what to study, when to study, and where to get help. By the time most students recognize they are struggling, they have already missed the optimal intervention window.

---

## Solution at Scale

A production-ready Academic Readiness Coach would:

1. Integrate with Ivy Tech's student information system to access real diagnostic assessment scores, enrollment records, and class schedules.
2. Provide each student a personalized weekly plan from Day 1 of the semester.
3. Alert instructors and academic coaches when a student's plan indicates very limited study availability combined with weak diagnostic scores (an objective support trigger, not a grade prediction).
4. Adapt weekly priorities as the semester progresses and new assessment data becomes available.
5. Connect directly to tutoring center booking systems, reducing friction from recommendation to appointment.

The following are **aspirational metrics**, not confirmed findings:

|---|---|---|
| Gateway math course success rate | ~52% (MATH 136, statewide estimate) | 65% |

## Important Limitations and Responsible AI Notes

**This prototype does not predict grades.** The readiness categories (Needs Attention, Developing Proficiency, Strong Foundation) are heuristics based on diagnostic scores. They are study-planning aids, not academic placement decisions.

**No recommendation replaces human advising.** The system is designed to lower the barrier to accessing human support — tutoring, office hours, coaching — not to substitute for it. Every recommendation surface includes a direct path to a human resource.

**Diagnostic scores may not reflect all relevant factors.** Test anxiety, life circumstances, and prior coursework not captured in the diagnostic are not visible to the system. Instructors and advisors should interpret recommendations in that fuller context.

**Fairness.** A production deployment would require ongoing monitoring for disparate recommendation patterns across demographic groups. No such analysis has been conducted on the fictional prototype data.

**Privacy.** A production system would require appropriate data governance, FERPA compliance, and informed consent processes before collecting or processing real student data.

## Connection to Ivy Tech 2030 Strategic Plan

This tool directly supports:

- **Gateway course success rate** — the primary metric — by providing students with structured, proactive academic support in their first semester.
- **Equitable student outcomes** — by ensuring every student receives the same quality of academic planning support regardless of their familiarity with college resources.
- **Faculty and advisor capacity** — by surfacing objective indicators so human advisors can focus their time on students with the highest support needs.
